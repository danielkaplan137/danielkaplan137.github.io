**The group is newly established at UCF and actively recruiting: two postdoctoral positions and one PhD student position.** Founding members shape the direction of a group in a way later arrivals cannot, and there is a lot of open ground.

To apply, email your **CV** to **[daniel.kaplan@ucf.edu](mailto:daniel.kaplan@ucf.edu)**. Please put the position and a specific topic in the subject line — it tells me immediately what to read your CV for.

## Postdoctoral Researchers — 2 positions

Open to candidates with a PhD in physics, materials science, or a closely related field, held or expected before the start date.

The two positions are aimed at different parts of the group's work, and applicants may express interest in either or both:

**Position 1 — Quantum geometry, nonlinear response, and topological phases.**
Analytical and many-body work on nonlinear optical and transport response, superconductivity from repulsive interactions, and topological crystalline states. Useful background: many-body perturbation theory, response function formalism, symmetry analysis of magnetic and crystallographic groups, moiré and multilayer systems.

**Position 2 — First-principles and computational materials theory.**
Large-scale electronic structure calculation applied to real materials, including downfolding to interacting lattice models and machine-learning-assisted materials prediction. Useful background: DFT (VASP, Quantum ESPRESSO), Wannier90, constrained RPA, DMFT, and scientific Python.

The division is not rigid. Several current projects sit squarely between the two, and a candidate strong in one area with genuine curiosity about the other is of more interest to me than a perfect match on paper.

**To apply,** send:

- Your CV, including a publication list
- A short statement (one page is plenty) of what you have worked on and what you would want to work on here — specific is better than comprehensive
- Names and contact details of two or three referees

Applications are reviewed as they arrive and the positions remain open until filled. Start dates are flexible.

## PhD Student — 1 position

One funded PhD position is available for a student entering through the [UCF Physics graduate program](https://sciences.ucf.edu/physics/graduate/).

Admission is handled by the department, not by individual faculty — so you must apply to the program directly. But do email me as well. Telling me you are applying and what interests you means I can look for your file, and it lets us talk about fit before decisions are made.

No prior research experience in condensed matter theory is required. Strong preparation in quantum mechanics and statistical mechanics matters, and comfort with programming matters a great deal — most of what we do involves writing code, whether that is a Monte Carlo sampler, a DFT workflow, or a kernel method. If you have taught yourself Python for something unrelated to physics, that counts.

**To apply,** email your CV, a transcript, and a short note about what you would like to work on. Then apply to the graduate program through UCF.

## Undergraduate Researchers

## Why here

UCF's Department of Physics has an active experimental condensed matter presence in quantum materials, including ARPES on topological systems, which makes for short-loop theory-experiment collaboration on the same campus. CREOL, UCF's College of Optics and Photonics, is one of the leading optics institutes in the country and sits within walking distance — an unusual advantage for a group working on nonlinear and ultrafast light-matter interaction.

The group also maintains long-running collaborations with experimental groups elsewhere, including those of W. Gao (NTU Singapore), W. Wu (Rutgers), A. Pasupathy (Columbia), A. Kogar (UCLA), S.-W. Cheong (Rutgers), and Yu He (Yale). Group members are expected to talk directly with experimentalists, and travel is supported.

Orlando is inexpensive relative to most places with a research university of this size, which is worth saying plainly — postdoc and student salaries go considerably further here than in the Northeast or the Bay Area.
